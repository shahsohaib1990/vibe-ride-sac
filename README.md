# Sacramento Luxury Vibes

Build a professional, luxury-feel website for a Sacramento, California limousine and 

chauffeur transportation company called "USA Limo Vibes".

BRAND & STYLE

- Premium, upscale, trustworthy — think executive car service meets nightlife party rental.

- Color palette: deep black/charcoal background sections combined with white space, 

  with a gold or deep burgundy accent color for CTAs and highlights. Avoid anything 

  that looks cheap, cartoonish, or overly colorful.

- Typography: modern serif or clean sans-serif for headings (confident, elegant), 

  simple sans-serif for body text.

- Use high-quality imagery placeholders for fleet vehicles at night/city backdrops.

PAGES / STRUCTURE

1. Home

   - Hero section with a strong headline (e.g. "Sacramento's Premier Limousine & Party Bus Service"), 

     a short subheadline, and an instant quote/booking form right in the hero 

     (fields: pickup date, pickup time, pickup location, drop-off location, event type, 

     number of passengers, name, phone).

   - "Our Fleet" preview section with cards for: Stretch Limousines, Party Buses, 

     Hummer Limos, SUVs, Sedans — each with an image, short description, passenger 

     capacity, and a "View Details" button.

   - "Services We Offer" section: Weddings, Airport Transfers (Sacramento International/SMF), 

     Prom & Homecoming, Corporate Travel, Wine Tours (Napa/Amador), Bachelor/Bachelorette 

     Parties, Concerts & Sporting Events, Quinceañeras, Anniversaries, Night Out/Club Hopping.

   - Trust/why-choose-us bar: Licensed & Insured, Professional Chauffeurs, 24/7 Availability, 

     On-Time Guarantee, Years in Business, Clean & Sanitized Vehicles.

   - Testimonials/reviews carousel with star ratings.

   - Service area section mentioning Sacramento, Elk Grove, Roseville, Folsom, Davis, 

     Rocklin, and surrounding areas.

   - Final CTA banner with phone number (click-to-call) and "Get Instant Quote" button.

   - Footer with contact info, social links, service links, and licensing/DOT number placeholder.

2. Fleet page

   - Individual cards/sections for each vehicle category (Stretch Limo, Party Bus, Hummer, 

     SUV, Sedan) with image gallery placeholders, passenger capacity, amenities 

     (LED lighting, sound system, bar, leather seating, TVs), and a "Request This Vehicle" button.

3. Services page (or individual service pages)

   - Each service type gets its own section/card with a short description and relevant CTA.

4. About Us

   - Company story, years serving Sacramento, fleet size, chauffeur training/professionalism, 

     licensing and insurance details, safety commitment.

5. Contact / Book Now

   - Full booking form (same fields as hero, plus special requests box)

   - Embedded map showing Sacramento service area

   - Phone, email, hours of operation

   - Click-to-call button prominent on mobile

GLOBAL ELEMENTS

- Sticky header with logo, nav (Home, Fleet, Services, About, Contact), and a prominent 

  "Book Now" button + phone number always visible.

- Mobile-first responsive design — large tap targets, click-to-call button fixed at 

  bottom on mobile.

- Fast-loading, clean animations on scroll (subtle fade/slide-ins, nothing distracting).

- SEO-friendly headings and structure (H1/H2 hierarchy), since this will later be 

  converted into a WordPress theme.

Keep the code structure clean and componentized (separate components for Hero, FleetCard, 

ServiceCard, Testimonial, BookingForm, Header, Footer) since this project will be exported 

and rebuilt as a WordPress theme afterward.
